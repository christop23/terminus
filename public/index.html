Terminus

The legend of the Cursed King has been whispered about for centuries, a cautionary tale of power and greed. It is said that he stumbled upon a mysterious artifact known as the Toxic Treasure, which brought him great wealth but also a terrible fate. Many believed that the treasure was tainted with dark magic, and that it slowly consumed the king's soul. As time went on, the king's downfall became apparent, leading some to wonder if the treasure was truly worth the price.
